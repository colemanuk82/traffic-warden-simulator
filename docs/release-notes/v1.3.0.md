## Traffic Warden Sim 1.3.0: "School Run Rampage"

Drop-off time at Parkside Primary, and the warden has had enough.

### Which file do I want?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.3.0-setup.exe** | Windows installer. Start Menu entry, desktop icon, uninstaller. |
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.3.0-windows-portable.zip** | Windows, no install. Unzip anywhere and run `TrafficWardenSim.exe`. |
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.3.0.apk** | Android. Open this page on your phone, tap it, install. Installs over 1.2.0 and keeps your progress. |

### New in this release

- **New level: School Patrol** (unlocks at 4 stars). Parents on the school run park on the lines and on the
  yellow **SCHOOL KEEP CLEAR** zig-zags (+50 a ticket). Kids hop out and run in through the gate. Two
  **lollipop ladies** walk out with their STOP signs and hold the traffic while the children cross. Whistle
  near one and she'll tell you that's her job. Parents have plenty to say: "Haven't you got anything better
  to do?!", but also "Glad you're here, they behave when you are!"
- **RAMPAGE MODE**: floor three drivers in a row (each within 15 seconds of the last) and you get a
  **baseball bat** for 15 seconds.
  - Your eyes glow red, the screen goes red with drips running down it, and a new heavy metal track,
    *Batter Up!*, takes over.
  - Batted drivers are knocked out cold and stay down until the rampage is over, in a red puddle. Walk
    through one and you leave a trail of red footprints.
  - Bat the cars: dents, cracked windscreens and flying bits. The third hit makes it **explode**: a fireball,
    a flip, a burnt-out shell on fire, and any drivers nearby knocked flat.
  - +40 per knockout, +25 per smash, +100 per wreck.
- **Phones: the Depot (shop) list now scrolls** when you drag it. The Unlockables list too.
- **The whistle is quieter.**
