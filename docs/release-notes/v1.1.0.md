## Traffic Warden Sim 1.1.0: "Pocket Warden"

The council have issued every warden a phone. Traffic Warden Sim now runs on **Android**.

### Which file do I want?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.1.0.apk** | The Android game. Download it on your phone and tap it to install. |

On Windows? Nothing changes for you, so keep playing [1.0.0](https://github.com/colemanuk82/traffic-warden-simulator/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

### Installing on your phone

1. Open this page on your phone and tap **TrafficWardenSim-1.1.0.apk**.
2. Open the download. Android asks to let your browser or Files app **install unknown apps**: allow it.
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.

Android 5 or newer with OpenGL ES 3.0. Phones with 64-bit and older 32-bit chips are both covered.

### New in this release

- **Touch controls** that only show up on a touch screen: a stick that appears wherever your left thumb lands
  (push it past the ring to run), and big **TICKET**, **PUNCH**, **WHISTLE**, **DIVE**, **RUN**, **GADGET**
  and **SWAP** buttons on the right. Slide a thumb from one button to another and it just works.
- A **pause button** at the top, and the phone's **back button** pauses a shift or goes back in menus.
  Switching away from the game pauses it too.
- **Tap menus** everywhere, with tappable **BACK** and tab prompts, and a Touch column on the Controls page.
- Fits **any phone shape** and keeps the buttons clear of notches and rounded corners.
- Tuned for **mid-range phones**: the lighter Compatibility renderer, the game drawn at 720p on Medium (the phone
  default) and Low, smaller shadows and a 60 fps cap. Pick **High** in Settings for full resolution.
- A Bluetooth controller works on the phone too, and the touch buttons hide while you use it.
