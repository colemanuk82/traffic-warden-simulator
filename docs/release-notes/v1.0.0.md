## Traffic Warden Sim 1.0.0: "Uniform On"

The council have finally signed off the budget. Get your high-vis on, Warden. It's release day.

![Traffic Warden Sim](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/colemanuk82/traffic-warden-simulator/main/docs/screenshots/title-screen.jpg)

### Which file do I want?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.0.0-setup.exe** | The installer. Start Menu entry, optional desktop icon, proper uninstaller. Pick this one if in doubt. |
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.0.0-windows-portable.zip** | No install. Unzip anywhere (even a USB stick) and run `TrafficWardenSim.exe`. |

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. No Godot or anything else needed.

Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed by a big company.
Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Honest, guv.

### What's on the beat

- **12 streets** from Pleasant Street to Neon City, a supermarket car park, a multi-storey, two very hilly villages, **Spooky Hollow** (Halloween) and **Christmas Eve**
- **4 special events**: Bin Strike, Wedding Day, Match Day and the Christmas Market
- **3 boss drivers**: the Lord Mayor and his bodyguards, White Van Man and the getaway car
- **24 voiced driver excuses**, every one of them a lie
- **Career ranks** from Trainee Warden to Chief Parking Enforcer, with wheel clamps, a tow truck and a camera van
- **The Depot**: Turbo Shoes, a Tea Flask, a Council Bike, and weapons including a brolly and a wet fish
- **Weather** that's out to get you: puddle-splashing drivers, lightning, snow, blizzards and fog
- **Split-screen two player**: Co-op or Ticket Race
- **Endless mode, daily and weekly challenges, Golden Tickets, high scores and medals**
- **Full Xbox controller support**

### New in this release

- **A tidier main menu**: everything now lives under **Single Player**, **Multiplayer**, **Unlockables** and **Scoreboard**
- New **Golden Tickets**, **Stars and Medals** and **Your Stats** pages
- A proper Windows **installer** and a **portable** build. No more batch files or downloading Godot
