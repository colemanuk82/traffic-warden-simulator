## Traffic Warden Sim 1.2.0: "Heavy Metal Warden"

The council's new radio budget went entirely on distortion pedals.

### Which file do I want?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.2.0-setup.exe** | Windows installer. Start Menu entry, desktop icon, uninstaller. |
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.2.0-windows-portable.zip** | Windows, no install. Unzip anywhere and run `TrafficWardenSim.exe`. |
| **TrafficWardenSim-1.2.0.apk** | Android. Open this page on your phone, tap it, install. Installs over 1.1.0 and keeps your progress. |

### New in this release

- **Death metal is the new default soundtrack.** Three death metal tracks with growled (family-friendly)
  vocals: *Double Yellow Death*, *Clamped in Darkness* and *Pay and Display of Doom*, plus a death metal mix
  of the menu theme. Existing saves switch over once; pick **Classic** in Settings to go back.
- **Ten more rock and metal styles**: thrash, doom, groove metal, symphonic black metal, metalcore, nu-metal,
  hardcore punk, grunge, stoner rock and glam rock. The **Rock & Metal** setting shuffles all of them, and
  the Jukebox now has 32 tracks. A new death metal car stereo rattles past too.
- **Ruder drivers** (still family-friendly): 29 new insults, each voiced by two men and two women.
  "You absolute plonker!", "Get stuffed, you plum!", "Robocop on a budget!", "Bwok bwok! Chicken man!"
- **Punching that lands when it should**:
  - Punches aim themselves at the nearest driver, whichever way you're facing, and you lunge in to connect.
  - A ring under the driver shows who you'd hit: yellow when they're in reach, green when it's the perfect
    moment. A **PERFECT PUNCH** gives +25, extra knock-back and a tiny freeze-frame.
  - Press a fraction early (or during the cooldown) and the punch waits for the driver to arrive.
  - On phones the **PUNCH** button is bigger, glows when a driver is in reach and flashes **NOW!** for the
    perfect moment.
  - In testing, a player with average timing floored three to four times as many charging drivers and got
    clobbered far less.
- **Automatic update check**: when a new version is out, a green **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button on the main menu
  takes you to the right download for your device. Quiet when offline, and you can switch it off in Settings.
