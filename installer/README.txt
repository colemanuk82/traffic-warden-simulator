TRAFFIC WARDEN SIM
==================

Right then, Warden. Double-click TrafficWardenSim.exe to start your shift.
No Godot, no faff, nothing else to install.

Controls (keyboard / Xbox pad)
  Move ................ WASD or arrows / left stick
  Sprint .............. Shift / LB or LT
  Write a ticket ...... E or left click / A (press again to stop the needle)
  Punch ............... F or right click / Y or RT
  Whistle ............. Space / X
  Dive ................ Ctrl / B
  Use gadget .......... G / RB      Switch gadget: Tab / View
  Pause ............... Esc or P / Menu

Your career, wages and wardrobe are saved in:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Traffic Warden Sim
